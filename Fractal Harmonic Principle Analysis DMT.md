Strip psilocybin of its phosphate, strip away every elaboration, and the irreducible resonator remaining is DMT. It is structurally the simplest psychedelic and, not coincidentally, the most intense: there is no ester to hydrolyze, no ring to fuse, no kinetic trap—just the root frequency of the series striking the 5-HT₂ₐ receptor directly. Critically, DMT is endogenous: it occurs in trace amounts in mammalian tissue, synthesized from tryptamine by INMT. The body already knows this harmonic. DMT is not an alien key introduced from outside—it is a latent frequency already present in the instrument, ordinarily damped below the threshold of perception.

  Impulse-Function Harmonics

  Where LSD demonstrates residence-time amplification, DMT reveals the opposite FHP principle: transient impulse response. DMT is degraded by monoamine oxidase almost as fast as it arrives—smoked, the entire arc is minutes. This is not a plateau but a delta-function: a near-instantaneous strike and an equally rapid decay.

  Smoked DMT:       onset ▓ ──────── offset       (minutes; pure impulse)
                       │                │
                       └─ MAO degrades ─┘  (no kinetic trap, instant decay)

  Ayahuasca:        DMT + β-carboline MAOI (harmine/harmaline/THH)
                    └── MAO inhibited → DMT survives gut → hours of resonance

  The ayahuasca synergy is the FHP insight made botanical: a second plant (Banisteriopsis caapi) contributes nothing psychoactive of its own—it removes the damper (MAO) so the DMT-bearing plant (Psychotria viridis) can sound. One plant is the tone, the other lifts the mute. Two lineages composing a single resonance through complementary subtraction and emission—the inverse of psilocybin's convergent evolution, here a divergent collaboration.

  Key Divergence: Endogeneity and Speed

  - Psilocybin/LSD/MDMA/ketamine: exogenous compounds introduced into the system.
  - DMT: endogenous—already synthesized in trace amounts by the body's own INMT, also a sigma-1 receptor ligand (a target with no role for the other psychedelics).

  DMT's divergence is ontological: it is the only compound here that the organism makes. This reframes the experience—not the introduction of a foreign harmonic but the amplification of a native one ordinarily held below threshold. The sigma-1 coupling (an endoplasmic-reticulum chaperone receptor) hints at a cellular-stress / signaling role beyond the serotonergic story.
