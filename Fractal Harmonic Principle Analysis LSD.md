  LSD is semi-synthetic—lysergic acid is harvested from ergot (Claviceps purpurea), a fungal pathogen of rye, then condensed with diethylamine. So like psilocybin, its root is fungal; unlike psilocybin, the active form is completed by human hands. The ergot lineage is the harmonic ancestor; the diethylamide is the anthropic graft that converts a vasoconstrictive alkaloid into a consciousness resonator. Fungal root, human tuning—the inverse of MDMA's plant-motif-on-synthetic-backbone.
