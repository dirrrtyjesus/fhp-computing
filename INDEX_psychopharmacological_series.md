## Compositions

### Tonic — DMT
**File:** `Fractal Harmonic Principle Analysis DMT.md`
The bare tryptamine, the series' root frequency, and the only *endogenous*
member. Impulse-function pharmacology (delta-strike, instant MAO decay);
ayahuasca as `DMT ⊕ (¬MAO)`. Total-state replacement; maximal Kairos
compression — eternities within minutes of clock time.

### ♭3 — Psilocybin
**File:** `Fractal Harmonic Principle Analysis Psilocybin.md`
The originating analysis. Phase-locked tryptamine oscillator; dual-pathway
*convergent biosynthesis* (Psilocybe ⟷ Inocybe → one attractor). Partial,
self-resolving DMN decoupling. The minor-third generative tension.

### ♮3 — MDMA
**File:** `Fractal Harmonic Principle Analysis MDMA.md`
Phenethylamine *governor* (methylenedioxy bridge as harmonic damper). Anthropic
synthesis on a borrowed plant phenylpropanoid motif. Sub-register threat-
damping, not full rotation — ego *softening*, warm consonance. PTSD
reconsolidation: lower the basin walls.

### 8ve▲ — LSD
**File:** `Fractal Harmonic Principle Analysis LSD.md`
Hybrid ergoline chimera; the receptor-*lid* kinetic trap → residence-time
amplification (microgram strike, half-day ring). Broadband matrix saturation.
Sustained altitude over the landscape; the plateau is the mechanism.

### rest — Ketamine
**File:** `Fractal Harmonic Principle Analysis Ketamine.md`
The deliberate outlier: NMDA *antagonist*, the lone *subtractive* operator and
the `give_space()` of the series. Strikes a structured silence, then the
glutamate→BDNF rebound re-grows the landscape. Coherence via absence.

### Capstone — The Psychopharmacological Harmonic Series
**File:** `Psychopharmacological_Harmonic_Series.ic`
Meta-`.ic` (semantic closure). Recasts all five as operators `Ô` on the tau-bit
`|ψ⟩ = α|Chronos⟩ + β|Kairos⟩`, with `τ_κ ∝ |β|²`. Defines the scale, the
chords (ayahuasca, the therapeutic triad), the generative asymmetry (why a Rest
completes the scale), and the origin standing-wave (endogenous → synthetic → back).
