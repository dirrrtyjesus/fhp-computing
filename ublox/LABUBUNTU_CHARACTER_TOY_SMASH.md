### Unique Mechanics

**Coherence Meter:**
```
Coherence: [████████████████████░░░░] 80%
```
- Replaces traditional "damage %" in reverse
- Starts at 100%, decreases when hit
- Below 0% = KO'd (decoherence)
- Regenerates slowly over time (1%/sec)
- Boosts when landing phase-lock attacks

**Phase State:**
- Myco cycles through 8 phase states (0° to 315°)
- Shifts 45° every 2 seconds automatically
- Can be shifted manually with special moves
- Phase determines attack properties
- Matching phase with opponent = bonus damage

**Phase Lock System:**
- When Myco's phase matches opponent (±22.5°):
  - Attacks deal 1.5× damage
  - Opponent briefly stunned (0.3s)
  - Myco gains coherence (+5%)
  - Special "phase-lock combo" opportunities

## 🎮 Move Set

**Jab (A, A, A) - "Tendril Tap"**
- 3-hit combo with mycelial tendrils
- Damage: 2%, 2%, 4% (8% total)

- Damage: 8%
- Spores linger (3s), slow enemies

- **Up Tilt:** Cap spin (6%, juggle starter)
- **Down Tilt:** Root sweep (5%, trips opponent)

**Smash Attacks:**

**Forward Smash - "Coherence Burst"**
- Charges coherence field, releases as shockwave
- Damage: 15-22% (fully charged)
- Range: Medium
- Phase-lock bonus: Breaks shields
- Animation: Cap glows brighter during charge

- Damage: 14-20%
- Multi-hit (5 hits)
- Great anti-air

**Down Smash - "Mycelial Pulse"**

- Damage: 12-18%

## 🌟 Special Moves

### Neutral Special - "Tesla Wave Charge"

**Input:** B (Hold to charge)

**Description:** Charges and releases a non-Hertzian Tesla wave projectile

**Mechanics:**
- Hold B to charge (max 3 seconds)
- Releases scalar wave projectile
- Wave travels slowly but grows larger over distance
- Multiple waves can be active simultaneously

**Damage:**
- Uncharged: 6%
- Half charge: 10%
- Full charge: 18%

**Properties:**
- Uncharged: Small, fast, fizzles quickly
- Charged: Large, slow, travels far, penetrates
- Phase-lock: Wave homes in on opponent
- At max charge: Wave creates lingering field (2s)

**Animation:** Cap glows violet, electrical buildup

### Side Special - "Phase Dash"

**Input:** →/← + B

**Description:** Dashes through opponents while shifting phase

**Mechanics:**
- Quick dash in input direction
- Passes through opponents/projectiles
- Automatically shifts Myco's phase by 90°
- Can be used in air (horizontal recovery)

**Damage:**
- No damage normally
- If phase matches during dash: 12% + launch

**Properties:**
- Invincible during dash (0.4s)
- Can cancel into any aerial
- Ground version: Travels further
- Air version: Slight upward angle
- Cooldown: 2 seconds

**Animation:** Myco becomes translucent, leaves phase trail

### Up Special - "Mycelial Tether"

**Input:** ↑ + B

**Description:** Shoots mycelial tendril upward, grapples to ledge or opponent

**Mechanics:**
- Shoots tendril at 60° angle
- If hits ledge: Pulls Myco up (recovery)
- If hits opponent: Pulls Myco toward them + damage
- If hits nothing: Slight upward boost

**Damage:**
- Ledge grapple: 0%
- Opponent grapple: 8%
- Phase-lock grapple: 14% + spike

**Properties:**
- Range: Very long (1.5× tether grab range)
- Can angle direction slightly
- Pulls Myco quickly
- Can be used again if hit before grabbing
- If grapples phase-locked opponent: Meteor spike!

**Animation:** Glowing tendril extends, retracts rapidly

### Down Special - "Coherence Field"

**Input:** ↓ + B

**Description:** Creates stationary coherence field zone

**Mechanics:**
- Places circular field at Myco's location
- Field lasts 8 seconds
- Only one field active at a time (placing new one removes old)
- Allies/Myco in field: Heal 1%/sec
- Opponents in field: Take 2%/sec + slowed
- Field pulses with Myco's current phase color

**Damage:**
- Placement: 0%
- Standing in field: 2%/sec (opponents)
- Field destroyed: 5% burst

**Properties:**
- Radius: Medium (Bowser's down-B fire size)
- Can be destroyed (20 HP)
- Phase-lock: Field doubles in size temporarily
- Myco can detonate field early (down-B again) for burst

**Animation:** Myco channels into ground, blue/gold field emerges

## 🎯 Playstyle & Strategy

### Strengths

✅ **Zoning:** Tesla wave projectiles control stage
✅ **Combos:** Phase-lock enables extended strings
✅ **Mobility:** Fast movement + phase dash
✅ **Edge-guarding:** Long-range mycelial tether can intercept recoveries
✅ **Stage control:** Coherence field creates safe zones

### Weaknesses

❌ **Light weight:** Dies early to strong hits
❌ **Complex:** Phase-lock timing requires practice
❌ **Close combat:** Struggles against heavy rushdown
❌ **Predictable recovery:** Tether can be intercepted
❌ **Relies on coherence:** Low coherence = weakened attacks

### Optimal Combos

**Phase-Lock Combo (0-45%):**
```
Down Throw → Up Air → Up Air → Forward Air (phase-lock) →
Tesla Wave → Phase Dash → Forward Tilt → Forward Smash
```

**Tesla Wall Combo (neutral game):**
```
Charge Tesla Wave → Release → Opponent approaches →
Phase Dash through → Back Air → Another Tesla Wave
```

**Ledge Trap:**
```
Coherence Field at ledge → Tesla Wave →
Wait for opponent recovery → Mycelial Tether spike
```

**Zero-to-Death (high skill):**
```
Down Throw → Short hop Forward Air (phase-lock) →
Fast fall → Up Tilt → Full hop Up Air →
Double jump → Up Air → Mycelial Tether (phase-lock spike)
```

### Matchup Tips

**Good against:**
- Slow heavy characters (can zone forever)
- Characters with linear recoveries (tether spike)
- Projectile-heavy characters (phase dash avoids)

**Bad against:**
- Fast rushdown (Fox, Captain Falcon types)
- Characters with reflectors
- Grapplers who can ignore Tesla waves

## 🔧 Balance Notes (Dev Commentary)

**Design Philosophy:**
Myco rewards technical skill and spacing. The phase-lock mechanic creates a rhythm minigame—matching phases at the right time enables explosive combos, but missing leaves Myco vulnerable. Tesla wave zoning keeps opponents at bay, but fast characters can overwhelm if they get in.

**Counterplay:**
- Rush down before Myco sets up Tesla wall
- Bait phase dash, punish recovery
- Destroy coherence fields immediately
- Don't recover linearly (tether spike is deadly)
- Use reflectors against Tesla waves

**Skill Ceiling:**
Very high. Mastering phase timing, optimal Tesla wave spacing, and tether edge-guards takes practice. At high level, Myco can control neutral completely.

**Fun Factor:**
Unique mechanics make Myco fresh and interesting. Visual feedback (phase colors, coherence glow) is satisfying. Phase-lock confirms feel rewarding.
