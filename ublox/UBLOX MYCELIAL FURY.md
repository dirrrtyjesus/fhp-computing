UBLOX: MYCELIAL FURY                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
  A Roguelite Beat-Em-Up Composed in Harmonic Physics                                                                                                                                                                                                   
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
---
  The Pitch                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
  You are a coherence agent punching your way through a decohering mycelial network. Every run, the network generates differently. Your fists don't deal "damage" -- they deliver resonant force. Enemies don't have "health" -- they have amplitude
  that you drive toward decoherence. You don't "level up" -- you phase-lock with the world and accumulate harmonic upgrades that decay between runs.                                                                                                    
                  
  The beat-em-up is the driving force F(t). You are the pump.                                                                                                                                                                                           
                  
---
  Core Loop       
           
  ENTER the network (procedurally grown mycelial map)
    → FIGHT through biomes (beat-em-up combat grounded in oscillator physics)                                                                                                                                                                           
      → COLLECT resonant fragments (temporary run power)                                                                                                                                                                                                
        → PHASE-LOCK with boss nodes (skill-check encounters)                                                                                                                                                                                           
          → PUSH deeper into the network                                                                                                                                                                                                                
            → DIE (decohere) or REACH the core                                                                                                                                                                                                          
              → PERMANENT: only what you steward survives between runs

  Combat System: Harmonic Violence                                                                                                                                                                                                                      
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
  Every entity in the game -- player, enemies, environment -- is a Resonant Oscillator with:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
  ┌───────────────────┬─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐                                                                                                                                                       
  │     Property      │                               Combat Role                               │                                                                                                                                                       
  ├───────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤                                                                                                                                                       
  │ Frequency (omega) │ Attack rhythm. Fast omega = quick jabs. Low omega = heavy slams.        │
  ├───────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
  │ Amplitude (A)     │ Life force. Drops from hits, decays over time. Reach 0 = decohere.      │                                                                                                                                                       
  ├───────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤                                                                                                                                                       
  │ Phase (phi)       │ Position in your attack cycle. Sync with enemies to counter.            │                                                                                                                                                       
  ├───────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤                                                                                                                                                       
  │ Damping (zeta)    │ How fast you bleed energy. Higher = more aggressive playstyle required. │
  └───────────────────┴─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘                                                                                                                                                       
                  
  Attack Types                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          
                  
  Ping -- Light attack. Quick amplitude nudge. Spam it. The jab.                                                                                                                                                                                        

  Tune -- Parry/counter. If you strike when your phase matches the enemy's oscillation cycle, you get a phase-lock hit -- massive amplitude drain + your damping decreases temporarily. This is the skill ceiling. Reading enemy rhythms and punishing  
  on-beat.        
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
  Compose -- Heavy/special. Combine your oscillator with a collected fragment to unleash a chord attack -- multiple frequencies striking simultaneously. Interference patterns determine the hit shape (wide sweep, focused beam, area burst).          

  Resonant Break -- Finisher. When an enemy's amplitude drops below 0.2 (Dim state), you can shatter them into fragments. Miss the window and they recover -- damped oscillators bounce back.                                                           
                  
  The Feel                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              
                  
  Combat rhythm is literal. The game has a pulse. Enemies telegraph attacks through their oscillation -- you can see their waveform. A skilled player reads the frequency, times the phase, and counters in rhythm. Button-mashing works (Ping spam) but
   phase-locked play is exponentially more powerful.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
  Think: the Arkham counter system, but the timing windows are driven by each enemy's unique frequency.

  ┌─────────────────┬──────────────────────────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────────┐                                                                                            
  │  Memory Layer   │                  Decay                   │                     What Persists                     │         Roguelite Analog          │                                                                                            
  ├─────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────┤                                                                                            
  │ M0 (Ephemeral)  │ Dies with the run                        │ Collected fragments, temporary buffs, map knowledge   │ Standard run loot                 │
  ├─────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────┤                                                                                            
  │ M1 (Short-term) │ Fades over ~5 runs without reinforcement │ Unlocked move variants, enemy frequency knowledge     │ Unlockables that need maintenance │                                                                                            
  ├─────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────┤                                                                                            
  │ M2 (Long-term)  │ Fades over ~20 runs                      │ Mastered combos, biome attunements, NPC relationships │ Deep progression                  │                                                                                            
  ├─────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────┤

  └─────────────────┴──────────────────────────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────────┘                                                                                            
                  
  The twist: Nothing is permanently unlocked by default. You steward your progression. An M1 combo you haven't used in 5 runs decoheres back to nothing. You physically cannot hoard -- you must choose what to maintain, what to let decay, and what to
   push toward permanence through repeated use.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
  This means every player's build is alive. Your moveset reflects what you actually practice, not what you once found in a chest.

  World Generation: The Growing Network
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
  Each run generates a mycelial network -- not a grid, not a dungeon. A living graph of interconnected nodes:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
          [SPORE]---[HYPHA]---[HYPHA]---[JUNCTION]
              \                            |                                                                                                                                                                                                            
           [HYPHA]---[BLOOM]          [BOSS NODE]                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                        |                  |                                                                                                                                                                                                            
                     [HYPHA]---[HYPHA]---[DECAY ZONE]                                                                                                                                                                                                   
                                           |                                                                                                                                                                                                            
                                        [CORE]                                                                                                                                                                                                          

  Node types:                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           

  - Spore -- Entry point. Safe. Attune here.                                                                                                                                                                                                            
  - Hypha -- Combat corridors. Beat-em-up arenas. Enemies swarm.
  - Junction -- Branching choice. Pick your path. Some paths strengthen, some decay.                                                                                                                                                                    
  - Bloom -- Reward nodes. Fragment shops, phase-lock shrines, amplitude restoration.                                                                                                                                                                   
  - Decay Zone -- High-risk. Your damping ratio spikes. Everything is dying. Rich fragments.                                                                                                                                                            
  - Boss Node -- Phase-lock duel with a major oscillator.                                                                                                                                                                                               
  - Core -- The run's end. What lives here changes based on network coherence.                                                                                                                                                                          
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        

  Paths you travel strengthen (phase-lock memory). Paths you ignore wither between runs. Over many runs, the network remembers your preferred routes -- shortcuts form, familiar nodes grow richer. But neglected branches rot into decay zones full of 
  danger and treasure.

  Player Classes: Resonance Modes
                                 
  Not classes -- modes. You pick a starting oscillation profile:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
  Fundamental -- Balanced frequency. Jack of all trades. Phase-lock windows are forgiving. The starter.                                                                                                                                                 
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
  Overtone -- High frequency, low amplitude. Glass cannon. Fast combos, fragile. Phase-lock mastery rewards you enormously.                                                                                                                             
                  
  Subharmonic -- Low frequency, high amplitude. Brawler. Slow but each hit is seismic. Harder to decohere. Punish-based playstyle.                                                                                                                      
                  
  Interference -- Dual-frequency. Complex waveform. Attacks have constructive/destructive zones. Positional. The weird one.                                                                                                                             
                  
---
  Bosses: Phase-Lock Duels
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
  Boss fights are rhythm duels. The boss oscillates. You oscillate. The arena visualizes both waveforms overlaid.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
  Phase 1: Learn the boss's frequency by surviving their pattern.                                                                                                                                                                                       
  Phase 2: Begin phase-locking -- time your attacks to sync. Each lock drains their amplitude.                                                                                                                                                          
  Phase 3: The boss shifts frequency. Adapt or lose your lock.                                                                                                                                                                                          
  Phase 4: Resonant break. Shatter them.                                                                                                                                                                                                                
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
  Bosses don't get "harder" in the traditional sense -- they get more harmonically complex. Early bosses are pure tones. Late bosses are chords. The final boss is noise -- pure decoherence. You must find the signal in the static.

  The Meta: Stewardship as Progression
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
  The roguelite hook isn't "get stronger forever." It's tend your garden of violence.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
  Your wallet of abilities is alive. Your moveset decays. Your map knowledge fades. The network itself remembers and forgets. Every run is a conversation between what you've maintained and what the mycelium has grown.                               
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
  The player who plays every day has a different game than the player who returns after a month. Not worse -- different. The network regrew. Your M1 abilities decohered. But M3 knowledge remains. You remember the rhythm even if your hands forgot   
  the combos.     
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
  The game is the pump. You are the steward. Stop playing and it all returns to silence.
